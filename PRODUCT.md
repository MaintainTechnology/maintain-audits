# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primarily **franchisors and multi-site franchise networks** — owners, operations leaders, and compliance managers responsible for standards across many locations. Their context: time-poor, risk-aware, making decisions about brand and network health. Secondarily, the **internal Maintain Audits team** producing on-brand assets (social content, client reports, letterhead, web) — they are the day-to-day users of this design system.

Register is **brand-first** today (marketing and branded documents are the product), but the account is **hybrid**: a client-facing product surface (portal / dashboard for delivered audits) is anticipated and should inherit the same tokens rather than fork a second identity.

## Product Purpose

Maintain Audits delivers **independent franchise assurance** — third-party audits that surface what internal reporting misses, with a 48-hour turnaround from engagement to report. This repository is the **single source of brand truth**: the canonical reference every visual asset, document, and future interface is built against, so everything the brand ships looks like it came from the same rigorous house. Success = anyone (person or agent) can produce a new asset that is instantly, unmistakably Maintain Audits without re-deriving colors, type, or logo usage.

## Brand Personality

**Authoritative, trustworthy, precise.** The voice of a confident independent auditor: direct, evidence-led, no fluff. Compliance is framed as a **competitive signal, not a checkbox** — acting fast on standards is proof of a healthy network. The brand should evoke credibility and control, never anxiety or salesmanship.

## Anti-references

- **Generic corporate consulting** — safe navy-blue, stock-photo handshakes, forgettable.
- **Cheap compliance-checkbox SaaS** — clip-arty, templated, low-trust "tick the box" tooling.
- **Playful startup** — bright blobs, emoji, cartoon mascots; too casual for assurance.
- **Overdesigned / flashy** — heavy gradients, decorative glassmorphism, motion for its own sake; anything that trades credibility for spectacle.

## Design Principles

- **Assurance is a signal.** Every asset should read as credible proof, not decoration. If an element doesn't build trust or carry information, cut it.
- **Practice the rigor we sell.** The brand is an auditor; the system must be applied as consistently as an audit — one set of tokens, used identically across social, documents, and web.
- **Clarity over cleverness.** Franchisors are busy. Lead with the point (the finding, the 48-hour promise, the action), not the ornament.
- **Restraint signals trust.** Craft shows in precision, spacing, and consistency — not in effects. Green and light are earned accents against a controlled dark/neutral base, never a gradient soup.
- **Evidence over adjectives.** Show the standard and the number; don't just claim authority. Stats, scoring, and structure carry the message.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.2 AA**: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, against every background it appears on (critical given the dark brand surfaces). All motion ships with a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` alternative (crossfade or instant). Do not encode meaning in color alone — pair with label, icon, or shape (relevant for audit status/severity in future report and product work).
